Kiến thức cần nhớ trước khi làm

1. Statements

S + said (that) + clause
  • am/is/are → was/were; have/has → had.
  • will → would; can → could.
  • now → then; today → that day; tomorrow → the next day.

2. Questions

asked + if/whether/WH-word + S + V
  • Không đảo trợ động từ trong câu gián tiếp.
  • Yes/No: dùng if/whether.
  • WH-question: giữ từ để hỏi.

3. Commands & requests

told/asked + O + (not) to + V
  • “Open the door.” → told me to open the door.
  • “Don't talk.” → told us not to talk.

4. Reporting verbs

advise • promise • invite • suggest • admit
  • advise + O + to V; promise + to V.
  • invite + O + to V; suggest + V-ing.
  • admit + V-ing.
